# Successful DTC apparel stores: the evidence

Researched 2026-11-15. Every figure links to its source. Most of these brands are private, so where only third-party estimates exist (Particl, Grips, ECDB) I've left them out or marked them **unverified**. Those estimates often disagree with each other by 5–10x. "Platform" is what I observed in each homepage's source on 2026-11-15: `cdn.shopify.com` means Shopify, and `_next/static` means a headless Next.js front end.

Model key: **Core** = basics that are always available. **Drops** = scheduled limited releases. **Both** = core plus drops, which is Shelby's model.

## Ranked shortlist: the 8 most relevant to Shelby's UI

The ranking weighs core plus drops (Shelby's model), traffic from social video to mobile, a similar price band, and how strong the evidence is.

1. **Elwood**: the playbook's own brand. Same core-blank-plus-drops model and price band, profitable and ~90% DTC.
2. **Represent**: audited, profitable core essentials plus a drop and collab cadence. The best premium core-plus-drops storefront to study.
3. **Kith**: the canonical drop calendar (Monday at 11am ET). Study its release-time, product-reveal and sold-out UI.
4. **Gymshark**: audited £607m, social and community-led, with a constant launch cadence. Study its mobile navigation and launch pages.
5. **True Classic**: about $500M lifetime from a core tee and paid social. Study its social-traffic landing to PDP to checkout path.
6. **Buck Mason**: a signature tee with 60% repeat revenue. Study how it presents the core tee: fit, fabric and restock.
7. **Taylor Stitch**: Workshop pre-orders are ~11% of revenue. Study it as the "notify me" and pre-order drop pattern.
8. **Skims**: core plus drops at more than $1B. Study its size and fit UI, colorway drops and mobile PDP.

Alternates: Comfrt (creator-video landing, but self-reported numbers) and Aimé Leon Dore (art direction).
